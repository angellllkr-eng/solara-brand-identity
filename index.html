Direction 1 — Radiant Precision

Typography: Geometric Sans — Inter Display Black / Bold. High x-height, perfect circles, engineered precision. Display Black for wordmarks & heroes · Bold for subheads · Regular/Medium for UI.

Palette: Void Navy #0A1628 · Radiant Gold #F5C518 · Lumen White · Precision Steel.

Direction 2 — Terra Lumen

Typography: Refined Serif (display) + warm clean sans (body/UI). Soft contrast, elegant yet grounded. Serif carries living-force / sun-as-nature story; sans keeps packaging and digital usable.

Palette: Deep Forest #2C3E2D · Sunset Terracotta #E8A87C · Warm Cream · Soft Gold · Sage Mist.

Competitive Type Positioning

Category reality (Goal Zero · Jackery · EcoFlow · Bluetti · Anker SOLIX): almost exclusively clean geometric / condensed sans-serifs. Goal Zero leads with Galaxie Polaris Condensed (all-caps heroes). Anker SOLIX uses DIN Next LT Pro. Others default to Inter/Montserrat-adjacent stacks. Serifs are effectively absent.

Radiant Precision

High overlap

Competes directly in the technical geometric zone. Wins on sharper gold + navy execution and Inter Display density.

Terra Lumen

Near-zero overlap

Unoccupied register. Refined serif + warm sans creates the only true premium outdoor-lifestyle type system in the set.

Premium Outdoor Lifestyle Positioning

Terra Lumen is deliberately aligned with the premium outdoor lifestyle register occupied by Patagonia, YETI, Peak Design and Arc’teryx — not pure technical power brands.

Type map: Closest to Patagonia (heritage serif + refined sans). Warmer and more organic than YETI, Peak Design or Arc’teryx. Currently the warmest, most lifestyle-oriented system available in portable solar.

Color Principles

Deep Forest anchors. Warm Cream breathes. Sunset Terracotta + Soft Gold used sparingly. Neutrals first; photography carries the color. Nature-sourced, never synthetic-bright.

Photography Principles

Full-bleed, high-craft, quiet moments preferred over extreme action. Texture, atmosphere and golden-hour light. Product lived-in, never isolated. People present but environment larger.

Overall Feel

Elevated outdoor living powered by the sun. Refined, warm, enduring. Craft and restraint over loud technical claims.

Dual System Use

Radiant Precision for product/tech surfaces. Terra Lumen for lifestyle storytelling, packaging and brand moments. Two registers, one brand.
