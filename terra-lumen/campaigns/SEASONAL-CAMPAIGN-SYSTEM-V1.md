# Terra Lumen — Seasonal Campaign System v1

**Status:** VERIFIED — founder-provided campaign architecture captured as an operational brand specification.

## 1. Seasonal philosophy

Terra Lumen is a seasonal energy brand: light changes, life changes, energy changes. Each season is a chapter with deliberate changes to color temperature, tone, product emphasis, movement and emotional resonance.

## 2. Master campaign set

| Season | Campaign | Narrative | Visual system | Product focus | Retail activation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spring | Rise With the Light | Emergence, returning light, first journeys | Cream + Sage, soft gold, gentle gradients, dew highlights | TL-40, TL-Glow Lantern, TL-Carry | Spring Rise corner, sage textiles, light diffusion demos, early bundles |
| Summer | Drift With the Sun | Movement, long days, continuous exploration | Terracotta + Cream, warm gold, high-sun shadows, soft haze | TL-80, TL-Cell Standard, TL-Seasonal String Lights | Terracotta displays, outdoor demos, Summer Drift bundles |
| Autumn | Quiet the Light | Settling, warmth becoming precious, intentional energy | Deep Forest + Terracotta, soft gold foil, warm shadows, slow fades | TL-120, TL-Cell Reserve, TL-Path Lights | Forest displays, lantern demos, Autumn Quiet cabin bundles |
| Winter | Glow Through the Dark | Endurance, scarce light, warm autonomy | Cream + Forest, minimal gold, soft diffusion, snow-quiet overlays | TL-120 Winter Edition, TL-Cell Reserve, TL-Glow Lantern Winter Glow | Cream + Forest zone, warm-light demos, Winter Glow bundles |

## 3. Content architecture

Every season runs three coordinated layers:

1. **Hero Story** — one cinematic seasonal narrative.
2. **Micro-Stories** — short lifestyle vignettes across cabins, vans, camps, coastlines, forests, deserts and mountains.
3. **Product Stories** — functional demonstrations expressed through warm lifestyle use.

## 4. Partnership logic

- **Spring:** cabin rentals, forest lodges, agritourism farms.
- **Summer:** van-life creators, coastal eco-brands, desert retreats.
- **Autumn:** cabin influencers, forest photographers, slow-living creators.
- **Winter:** mountain lodges, winter survival creators, snow-quiet storytellers.

## 5. Retail behavior

Each seasonal change modifies scent, lighting temperature, textile palette, product emphasis and staff storytelling while retaining the core Terra Lumen identity.

## 6. Operating rule

Seasonal execution must remain coherent across **brand, retail, product, social, partnerships and content**. A seasonal campaign is complete only when its narrative, visual treatment, product tie-ins, retail behavior and content cadence agree.

## 7. Campaign release gate

Before release, verify:

- campaign name and narrative
- seasonal visual tokens
- product availability and SKU mapping
- retail activation assets
- hero/micro/product content briefs
- partnership targets
- social publishing cadence
- seasonal merchandising and staff guidance

No campaign should be marked live from a strategy document alone; execution assets and publishing/retail evidence are required.
