# High-Mass Dynamics & Heavy Asset Strategy

**Status:** Review draft — not approved for release  
**Owner repo:** `angellllkr-eng/solara-brand-identity`  
**Scope boundary:** This project is isolated under `projects/high-mass-dynamics-heavy-asset-strategy/`. It does not alter the SOLARA public site, deployment contract, or production workflows.

## Objective

Create a source-backed editorial infographic connecting four distinct systems:
1. High-performance vehicle engineering: mass, aero load, tires and braking.
2. Heavy-vehicle stopping-distance fundamentals.
3. Asset/credit-market context: dated CMBS and ABS index yields.
4. Decision discipline: separate measured facts, model outputs and unverified claims.

The visual must not imply that vehicle engineering data and fixed-income yields are directly comparable. They are separate panels connected only by the shared idea of mass, risk and evidence.

## Design direction

Use SOLARA's premium editorial discipline, not its product/commerce imagery: warm ivory canvas, near-black ink, restrained sun-gold accent, thin technical rules, large typographic numerals, compact source labels, and generous negative space. The project must remain visually independent of the existing Terra Lumen retail site.

## Research rules

- Cite primary manufacturer specifications wherever possible.
- State exact model/configuration and units beside every performance figure.
- Distinguish dry weight, curb weight, unsprung mass, axle downforce and total downforce.
- Label market yield observations with index name, geography, source and as-of date.
- Never present yield as guaranteed return or investment advice.
- Heavy-vehicle stopping equations are explanatory models only; real stopping distance depends on speed, grade, friction, brake condition, load, reaction time and operating conditions.
- Unsupported original-artwork claims are omitted or explicitly marked unverified.
